Methods

We evaluate our approach on five datasets spanning natural images, digits and

Results

Our method improves accuracy by 12% over the baseline across all evaluated
datasets. Gains are consistent and largest on SVHN.

Discussion

The approach is also more robust to input noise, which we attribute to the
